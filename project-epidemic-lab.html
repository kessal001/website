Epidemic Lab

An interactive SIR epidemic simulator that turns a system of differential equations into a visual, explorable learning experience in the browser.

The problem

Mathematical models are precise, but rarely tangible.

The SIR model is compact on paper, yet its dynamics become clearer when parameters can be changed and their consequences observed. A teaching tool must preserve the mathematics while making thresholds and the epidemic peak visible.

The approach

Make every assumption observable.

The application exposes population, initial conditions, transmission and removal rates. It integrates the equations numerically, redraws the curves, calculates R₀ and the infection peak, and explains both the model and its limits.

B

From equations to interaction

A model designed to be explored

  1. 01

    Simulate

    Integrate the SIR system with a fourth-order Runge–Kutta method and plot its compartments.

  2. 02

    Experiment

    Change initial conditions, β, γ and duration or begin from a preset scenario.

  3. 03

    Interpret

    Relate the reproduction number and infection peak to the parameters.

  4. 04

    Learn safely

    Run locally with no account or database while clearly stating the scientific limits of SIR.

Originally a 2020 school project, rebuilt in 2026 from an obsolete prototype into a responsive application for contemporary browsers.

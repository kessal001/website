The question

How much operating quality is already priced in?

Bending Spoons combines strong historical growth and margins with an acquisition-led model, financing needs and integration risk. The analytical task was to separate the quality of the platform from the expectations embedded in the market price.

The approach

Build the conclusion from traceable assumptions.

The model reconstructs audited 2023—2025 financials, incorporates Q2 2026 results and company guidance, forecasts five years, and triangulates value through DCF, trading comparables, reverse DCF, sensitivities and scenarios. The announced Airtable transaction is kept separate as an illustrative pro forma case.

D

Research process

From primary sources to an actionable thesis

  1. 01

    Source

    Anchor historicals and operating evidence to SEC filings, interim statements, company guidance and dated market data.

  2. 02

    Model

    Connect revenue, margins, reinvestment, capital structure and cash flows in a five-year forecast with explicit checks.

  3. 03

    Triangulate

    Compare intrinsic value, market multiples, implied expectations and bear-to-bull scenarios rather than relying on one output.

  4. 04

    Communicate

    Translate the full model into a 14-page report and a one-page thesis with a clear conclusion, catalysts and risks.

Dated conclusion

Underperform / wait for proof.

The conclusion is a historical snapshot, not a live recommendation.

Market price: $43.59

Blended target: $30

Implied downside: approximately 31%

Base DCF: $26 · Comps: $37

The workbook contains 102 formula cells, no external workbook links and eight model checks recorded as passing. The project is independent educational research for portfolio and recruiting purposes, not personalized investment advice.
